Link Analysis
How to use API docs

Analyse any business website

Paste a company's website link and get a report on its competitors, what customers search for, how to rank better on Google, its main services and who its customers are.

You can type it with or without https://. Takes about 20–40 seconds.

Or try an example:

How to use it

  1. 1
    Paste a website link

    Any company's homepage, e.g. razorpay.com, or click one of the examples above.

  2. 2
    Click “Analyse website”

    The tool reads the homepage and up to 3 key pages (About, Services, Pricing), then writes the report. Keep this tab open.

  3. 3
    Read the report

    Start with the summary at the top, then open the 5 numbered sections. You can download the result or save it as a PDF.

What you get

1Competitors

Who the business competes with, their strengths and weaknesses, and where it can win.

2Search queries

5 things customers would type on Google, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook or X to find it.

3SEO

How to rank higher on Google: better keywords, page title and description, plus a technical check-up.

4Services

The 5 most important things the business sells, and who each one is for.

5Audience

Who the customers are, what they need, and which groups to target in marketing.
